Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

TransApple Technologies collects only the information needed to provide our services — such as student, staff, and school records entered into Nova School — and contact details submitted through this website. We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure and is only accessible to authorised school staff and TransApple support personnel. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your school's data at any time by contacting us at hello@transappletechnologies.com.
