Trust & Security

Student data deserves serious protection.

We know schools are trusting us with sensitive records. Here's how we take that responsibility seriously.

Encrypted by Default

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, so records stay unreadable to anyone without authorised access.

Automatic Backups

School records are backed up continuously, so a lost device or power cut never means lost data.

Role-Based Access

Staff only see what their role permits — a bursar can't edit exam results, and vice versa.

You Own Your Data

Your school's records belong to your school. We never sell data, and you can request a full export anytime.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

TransApple Technologies collects only the information needed to provide our services — such as student, staff, and school records entered into Nova School — and contact details submitted through this website. We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure and is only accessible to authorised school staff and TransApple support personnel. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your school's data at any time by contacting us at transappletech@gmail.com.
